Privacy Policy

App: Hymn Book
Last updated: November 2026

Hymn Book is an offline Android application. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

Hymn Book does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files, and does not require an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

All hymn content is stored inside the app. Anything the app keeps on your device stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Sharing

The app lets you share hymn content using your device's built-in share menu. This only happens when you choose to do it, and what you share and who you share it with is entirely up to you. We do not receive any of it.

Third parties

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
